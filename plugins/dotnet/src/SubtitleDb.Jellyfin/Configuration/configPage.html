TSDb

Subtitles from the SubtitleDB open index. There is no account and no key, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to paste here.

Turn it on for each library under Dashboard, Libraries, Manage library, Subtitle Downloads, and pick the languages you want there: this plugin answers in whichever language Jellyfin asks for.

Follows the library's subtitle download settings, and saves even when the video has subtitles. A subtitle found when you press play is listed straight away.
Each download also gets a SYNCED - TSDb copy, lined up against a text subtitle inside the video. It reads "(syncing)" until done, and is removed if it cannot be lined up.
The same, lined up against the speech in eight minutes of the video's audio.
The most to offer for each language. Past 100, each further 100 is one more request.
Leave this alone unless you run your own mirror of the API.
