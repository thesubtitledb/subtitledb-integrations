SubtitleDB

Subtitles from the SubtitleDB open index. There is no account, no key and no quota, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to paste here.

Turn it on for each library under Settings, Library, with Show advanced settings on, and pick the download languages you want there: this plugin answers in whichever language Emby asks for.

Follows the library's subtitle download settings, and saves even when the video has subtitles. A new subtitle is listed from the next play.
The most to offer for each language. Past 100, each further 100 is one more request.
Leave this alone unless you run your own mirror of the API.
